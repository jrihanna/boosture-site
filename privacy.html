Boosture Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

Boosture is a virtual reality app for Meta Quest where you practise everyday social conversations with AI-driven characters. This policy explains what information Boosture uses, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

Boosture is operated by Reihanesadat Zekri, trading as Delgarm, in New South Wales, Australia ("we", "us"). You can contact us at rihanna.zekri.dev@gmail.com.

The short version. Boosture has no accounts and no ads. To let characters respond to what you say, short recordings of your voice are sent to our server, turned into text, and used to generate the character's reply. We don't sell your information, and we don't keep your recordings or conversations after they have been processed. Hand tracking stays on your headset.

1. Information Boosture uses

Information What it's used for Where it goes
Voice recordings (only while a scenario is listening for your reply) Converted to text so the character can respond to what you actually said. Sent over an encrypted connection to our server, then to our speech-to-text provider (ElevenLabs).
Conversation text (transcripts of what you said and the character's lines) Generating the character's next line, and the feedback summary at the end of a session. Our server and our AI provider (Anthropic). Character lines are also sent to ElevenLabs to generate the character's voice.
Hand tracking data Interacting with menus and buttons, and recognising the "raise your hand" help gesture. Processed on your headset only. Never stored or sent to us.
App settings (for example your chosen difficulty) Remembering your preferences between sessions. Stored on your headset only.
Technical data (IP address, request times, error and timing logs) Running the service, keeping it secure, fixing problems and measuring response times. Our server host (Google Cloud / Firebase).

We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or payment details, and Boosture does not use your Meta account profile.

2. When the microphone is on

Boosture asks for microphone permission the first time you start it. Your voice is only captured while a practice scenario is waiting for your reply. Recording pauses while the character is speaking, and anything picked up while the app is in the background (for example while your headset is off or the system menu is open) is discarded. You can turn off microphone access at any time in your headset's settings; the scenarios will still run, but characters won't be able to respond to what you say.

3. How long we keep information

  • Voice recordings and conversation text are processed to produce a reply and are not stored by us afterwards. During a session, the conversation is held in your headset's memory so the character can follow the conversation; it is cleared when you restart the scenario or close the app.
  • Server logs (technical data) are kept for up to 30 days and then deleted.
  • Our service providers may keep data for a limited period under their own policies, as described below.

4. Who we share information with

We only share information with the service providers that make Boosture work, and only what they need:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase – hosts our server (United States). Firebase privacy information
  • ElevenLabs – speech-to-text for your voice and text-to-speech for character voices. ElevenLabs privacy policy
  • Anthropic – the AI model that writes character lines and session feedback. Anthropic privacy policy

We use these providers through their business (API) services. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. International transfers

Our server and providers are located outside Australia, mainly in the United States. By using Boosture, your voice recordings and conversation text will be processed there. We choose providers that protect data in transit with encryption and that commit to protecting the information they process for us.

6. Not therapy or medical advice

Boosture is a tool for practising conversations. It is not therapy, counselling or a medical service, and it is not designed to diagnose or treat any condition. Please don't share sensitive personal information (such as health details) during practice conversations, since anything you say is processed as described above.

7. Children

Boosture is intended for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used Boosture, contact us and we'll help.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can stop voice processing at any time by turning off microphone permission or closing the app.
  • You can delete your on-device settings by uninstalling Boosture.
  • Because we don't keep recordings or conversations and have no accounts, we usually hold nothing that identifies you. If you have a question about your information, want access to it, or want it corrected or deleted, email us and we'll respond within 30 days.
  • If you're in Australia and unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC). If you're elsewhere, you may have similar rights under your local law.

9. Security

All communication between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Our provider API keys are kept on the server and are never stored in the app. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect so there is as little as possible to protect.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how Boosture handles information, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If the change is significant (for example, if we start storing conversations), we'll also tell you in the app before it takes effect.
