Boosture

Practise the conversations you dread, out loud, in VR. Speak up to a rude barista or push back on an unfair review, and get help the moment you freeze.

In development. Coming to Meta Quest.

How it works

1Pick a situation

Each scenario puts you face to face with a character in a realistic setting, from a busy café to your manager's office.

2Just talk

Say what you'd say in real life. The character listens and responds to what you actually said, so no two conversations go the same way.

3Get support if you freeze

Go quiet and help steps in gradually: a nudge, then a hint, then example phrases. Or raise your hand to ask for help straight away.

4See how it went

After each session you get friendly feedback on what worked and what to try next time. Then go again.

Who it's for

Anyone who finds certain conversations hard to start, or hard to stay in: introverts, people who replay awkward moments for days, or anyone who'd rather rehearse before the real thing. No audience, no judgement, as many tries as you like.

Scenarios

The café

Your order is wrong and the barista is in a hurry. Practise speaking up for something small.

The performance review

Your manager delivers an unfair review. Practise asking for specifics and holding your ground.

More scenarios are on the way.

Boosture is a tool for practising conversations. It is not therapy, counselling or a medical service.
